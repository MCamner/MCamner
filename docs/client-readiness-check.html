This first version is designed to be opened directly from an IGEL OS 12 or eLux 7 client. It cannot read protected OS settings on its own, but it can validate browser-visible signals, show likely client family, test basic readiness, and generate a report for support or operations.

Checks

These checks stay inside the browser sandbox. They are useful for first-line readiness validation, but not for deep policy inspection. For IGEL UMS, Scout/eLux, or local client settings, this page should later be paired with a management backend or local helper.

What this version can do

  • Detect likely IGEL, eLux, or generic Linux client family from browser-visible signals.
  • Check browser basics such as HTTPS, online state, resolution, locale, timezone, and viewport.
  • Run simple endpoint reachability checks to internal URLs you define in the page config.
  • Generate a compact report that can be copied into support tickets or troubleshooting notes.

What it cannot do alone

  • Read protected IGEL OS 12 or eLux 7 policy values directly from the operating system.
  • Verify Citrix Workspace configuration, certificates, or middleware beyond browser-visible behavior.
  • Inspect local device settings unless a helper service or management integration is added later.
